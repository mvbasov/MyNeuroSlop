Soroban Tutorial

About the Soroban

The soroban is a Japanese abacus. People use it to add, subtract, multiply, and divide numbers.

The soroban has a frame with many rods. Each rod holds five beads. The beads sit above and below a bar. This bar is the reckoning bar.

One bead sits above the bar. This bead is the heaven bead. It has a value of 5.

Four beads sit below the bar. These beads are the earth beads. Each earth bead has a value of 1.

To show a value on a rod, move beads toward the bar. A bead that touches the bar is active. Add the values of the active beads on a rod. This sum is the digit for that rod.

The rod on the right is the ones rod. Each rod to the left has a value ten times higher than the rod before it. A small dot marks every third rod, to help you read place value.

Click a bead to move it toward the bar. Click an active bead to move it away from the bar again.
